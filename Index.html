Foundations of Health at 40

Sleep

Adequate, consistent sleep supports recovery, cognition, mood and metabolic health.

Resistance training

Progressive strength training supports muscle, bone and functional capacity.

Aerobic fitness

Regular cardiovascular activity supports heart and metabolic health.

Nutrition

Build dietary changes around adequate protein, fiber, micronutrients and hydration.

Stress regulation

Breathing exercises, relaxation, music, visualization and other behavioral strategies may complement a broader health program.

Movement & mobility

Maintain strength, mobility, balance and useful movement capacity.
